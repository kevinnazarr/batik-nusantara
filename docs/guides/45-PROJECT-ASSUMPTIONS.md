# 45. Project Assumptions & Open Decisions

## Current assumptions

- Single merchant/store, not marketplace.
- Indonesia-focused launch.
- IDR currency.
- Guest checkout supported.
- Product stock is SKU/variant based.
- One primary fulfillment location for MVP.
- Biteship is the shipping abstraction; individual couriers remain provider options.

## Open decisions before production

- Final payment methods enabled in Midtrans account.
- Final courier/service matrix and origin address.
- Email provider and sending domain.
- Exact returns/refunds policy.
- Final tax/invoice requirements.
- Final VPS sizing and backup storage.
- Final brand typography and palette.
