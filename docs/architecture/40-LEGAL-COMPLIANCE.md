# 40. Legal & Operational Compliance Checklist

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Validate final copy/policy with the business and applicable Indonesian requirements.

Required site surfaces:

- Terms & Conditions
- Privacy Policy
- Shipping Policy
- Return/Refund Policy
- Contact/customer service information

Operationally document what customer data is collected, retention, provider sharing, cookies/analytics usage, and how refund/support requests are handled.
